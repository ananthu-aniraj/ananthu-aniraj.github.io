I am excited to announce that our paper "Two-stage Vision Transformers and Hard Masking offer Robust Object Representations" has been accepted to appear at the [ICPR 2026](https://www.icpr2026.org/) conference as an oral presentation. The preprint is available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08915). I would like to thank my co-authors and collaborators for their contributions to this work, and I look forward to sharing our findings with the community at ICPR 2026!
